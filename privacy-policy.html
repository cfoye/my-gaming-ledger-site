My Gaming Ledger Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

My Gaming Ledger is a personal record of your casino trips. We do not collect your data. The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics or tracking, and we run no server that receives your information.

What the app stores

The app stores what you enter: trips, sessions, games, casinos, amounts, markers, notes and photos. It is saved on your iPhone.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, your records sync to the private database in your own iCloud account so they can be restored or used on another device. We cannot access that database. Apple's handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Face ID or passcode locks the app. Apple handles this check, and the app never receives your face data or passcode.
  • Camera is used only when you choose to take a photo.
  • Photos are used only when you choose a picture to attach, or tap Save to Photos on a summary picture.

Sharing and exports

Information leaves the app only when you choose to share a summary picture or export a CSV file. Whatever app or person you send it to handles it under their own terms.

Deleting your data

You can delete trips, sessions, markers and photos in the app, and those deletions sync to your iCloud. Deleting the app removes its data from the iPhone. To remove the copy in iCloud, open Settings, tap your name, then iCloud, and manage the storage for My Gaming Ledger.

Age

The app is intended for adults of legal gambling age where they live. It does not offer gambling or real-money wagering.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
