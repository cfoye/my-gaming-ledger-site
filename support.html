How is win/loss calculated?

Each finished session's result is what you left with minus your total in. Total in includes what you sat down with, money you added, and markers taken during that session. Sessions still in progress aren't counted.

Are markers counted as a loss?

No. Markers are tracked separately as debt. A marker taken during a session counts as money in for that session, and paying it back never changes a result.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same iCloud account and make sure iCloud is on for the app. Your records sync after you install it. Syncing can take a few minutes.

How do I keep my own copy?

Go to Reports, then Export Data, to create CSV files you can save or email to yourself.
